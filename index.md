# HomeAssistantEMS-2027 – Terms and Conditions

**Last updated: 5 November 2026**

## 1. Purpose

HomeAssistantEMS-2027 is a private, non-commercial application connecting a personally owned Volvo XC40 Electric to Home Assistant, an open-source home automation platform.

The application is intended exclusively for personal home automation and energy management.

## 2. Use of Vehicle Data

The application may access vehicle information, including battery state of charge, charging status, connection status and climate control functions, subject to permissions granted by the vehicle owner.

This information is used to monitor the vehicle and coordinate electric vehicle charging with residential electricity consumption, solar energy production and battery storage.

## 3. Access and Authorization

The application is intended solely for the developer's personal use with their own Volvo vehicle.

Vehicle access requires authorization through Volvo ID. Authorization may be revoked through the relevant Volvo account controls.

## 4. Data Protection

Vehicle information is used for personal home automation purposes.

The application does not sell vehicle data or provide vehicle information to third parties for commercial purposes.

Data may be processed by Home Assistant and the connected service providers required for its operation, subject to their respective terms and privacy practices.

## 5. Availability and Liability

The application is provided for personal use without any guarantee of continuous availability or uninterrupted operation.

It is not intended for safety-critical vehicle control.

These terms may be updated if the application's functionality or data handling changes.
